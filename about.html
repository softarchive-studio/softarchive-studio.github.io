About Soft Archive

A place for
looking closer.

Soft Archive began with a simple question: why do some ordinary things stay in our memory long after we have stopped using them?

Why This Archive

Not everything memorable has to be extraordinary.

A notebook we carried for years. A cup that somehow became our favourite. A shop we still remember after visiting once.

Sometimes it is the smallest details that become connected to a particular period of our lives.

Soft Archive is a place to slow down and look at those details again.

What I Collect

Objects, places, and the ideas behind them.

The archive moves between stationery, tableware, lifestyle objects, independent shops, materials, and everyday rituals.

  • Stationery & Writing
  • Home & Tableware
  • Lifestyle Objects
  • Independent Design
  • Places & Small Discoveries
How I Choose

Curiosity comes first.

Soft Archive does not try to collect everything that is new or popular.

Instead, each collection begins with something that makes me stop for a moment: a particular material, a useful detail, an unusual way of presenting an everyday object, or simply a feeling that stays afterwards.

The archive is personal by nature. What appears here is shaped by curiosity, observation, and the things I would want to remember.

A Small Note
